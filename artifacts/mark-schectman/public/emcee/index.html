Meet Your Host

Mark Schectman

Mark Schectman is a Dallas–Fort Worth corporate event emcee and host with 20+ years behind the mic — an award-winning broadcaster who's guided everything from intimate executive experiences to festival crowds of 22,000.

He's hosted the CultureMap Tastemaker Awards, the World Affairs Council's H. Neil Mallon Award Dinner, the PRSA Dallas Pegasus Awards, EdgeFest, Red Bull Sound Select, and the Gold's Gym International Convention — and lends his voice to Audible and formerly Smithsonian Magazine.
